We do not collect any directly identifiable personal information (such as your name, home address, or phone number) unless you explicitly provide it to us via direct support communications.

Depending on the specific features of the Application, the app may request access to certain system permissions (e.g., Audio/Microphone settings or System Volume controls to process speaker/audio features). These permissions are strictly executed locally on your physical device and are never recorded or transmitted to our external servers.
